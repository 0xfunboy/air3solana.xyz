Protocol

The vault protocol in five moves.

Epoch-based on-chain execution with full profit distribution. The AIR3 agent scans approved signals and executes automated perp trades on Pacifica; trade size adapts to vault liquidity, so PnL is tracked as a size-weighted average across all participants.

DepositUsers deposit USDC into the vault. Receipt units are minted to the wallet immediately.
StrategyThe agent executes trades on Pacifica via the executor wallet and builder code.
Season closeThe weekly season settles and net PnL is finalized on schedule.
Profit splitPositive PnL routes through treasury fee, AIR3 buyback and the 50/50 split.
ExitBook exit, wait one season rollover, then claim capital and accrued rewards.
Season cycle

Everything moves on a weekly clock.

Your capital always follows the same four stages. All capital movements, from activation to settlement to exits, happen at the weekly cutoff.

PendingDeposited capital waiting for the next season. It does not trade yet.
ActiveCapital live in the current season, exposed to weekly PnL.
Exit bookedWithdrawal requested. Keeps trading until the season settles.
ClaimableExit cycle finished, ready to claim to your wallet.
At each weekly cutoff
  1. The running season settlesPnL is finalized for all active capital.
  2. Positive PnL is extractedRouted to the reward flow: treasury, buyback, distribution.
  3. Pending deposits activateThey join the new season and start trading.
  4. Booked exits move outInto the isolated claimable reserve.
  5. A new season startsThe countdown resets for one more week.
