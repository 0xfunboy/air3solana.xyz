Product

What is AIR3?

AIR3 is an execution-native AI trading agent on Solana built by AIRewardrop. It combines live market intelligence, social distribution, AIRTrading vault execution, AIRTrack proof, AIRi voice/avatar interaction and AIR3 buyback/distribution flows in one product system.

What is AIRdApp?

AIRdApp is the operational hub where users access AIRTrack, AIRTrading, AIRSocial, AIRSponsor and AIRTool with one Solana wallet connection: airdapp.airewardrop.xyz ↗

What is AIRTrading?

The vault-based autotrading module: users deposit USDC into a shared on-chain vault, the AIR3 agent executes approved perp trades on Pacifica, and results settle weekly at the end of each season. See AIRTrading and the User Manual.

What is AIRTrack?

The proof layer: it records trade states (pending, open, closed, discarded), realized PnL and full performance history so users can verify what happened instead of trusting claims. AIRTrack has no deposits and no seasons: it is the pure trading record.

What is AIRi?

The embodied interface layer: a wallet-authenticated voice/chat/3D-avatar session with market context and execution-ready UX, in browser and mobile. It debuted as AIRifica during the Pacifica Hackathon and is now simply AIRi: airi.airewardrop.xyz ↗

Is AIR3 already live?

Yes. The token (Nov 2024), the social/live agent, AIRdApp, AIRTrack and the AIRTrading mainnet beta are all in production. The launch phase of the roadmap is complete: see the updated roadmap.

Deposits & seasons

What is a season?

One weekly trading cycle. All capital movements (deposit activation, settlement, exits) happen at the weekly cutoff. The dashboard shows the countdown at all times.

What are the deposit limits?

Currently 10 to 500 USDC per wallet during mainnet beta. Limits may change as the vault matures; the values shown in the dApp panels are always the ones in force.

What is the holder threshold?

When the whitelist policy is ON, listed wallets can deposit; holding at least the AIR3 threshold (currently 1,000,000 AIR3) also qualifies. Check the Deposit panel in the dApp for the live values.

Why do deposits activate later?

Deposits enter as pending and activate at the next weekly cutoff so all capital follows the same season accounting model. Pending capital does not trade, does not earn weekly PnL, and is not routed to the executor yet.

Do new deposits inherit old losses?

No. New deposits enter at the next global season index and only start counting PnL from the season they activate in.

Exit, claim & rewards

Why is exit delayed?

Booked capital keeps participating until the current season settles, then moves into the isolated claimable reserve when the next season opens. Worst case, the full path takes about two weekly cutoffs.

How does the buyback work?

At settlement, 1% of positive weekly PnL goes to treasury (min $0.10); the remainder buys AIR3 on the market if it reaches the $5 minimum swap threshold. Bought AIR3 splits 50% to users pro rata and 50% to treasury.

Where are my AIR3 rewards delivered?

They accrue at each profitable settlement and are delivered when you claim.

Can I lose money?

Yes. The agent trades perps and seasons can close in loss. Losses reduce the season index and therefore your capital. Only deposit what you can afford to risk. Read the risk disclosure.

Execution & trust

How will leverage scale?

In controlled steps toward 10x, on a strategy that has already proven itself. The Backtest Engine replays the live strategy on real datasets and validates every config before it reaches execution; as live and simulated results converge, size steps up. Leverage multiplies validated performance, so validation comes first.

What is the simulation vs live gap?

AIRTrack runs strategy simulation alongside live execution and measures the distance between the two. Closing that gap is the alignment metric that gates each leverage step; the Backtest Engine works on the same real dataset to keep configs honest.
