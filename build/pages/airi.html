AIRi in action: avatar, voice and execution-ready UX in the browser.

Per-user instanced avatar

Each user gets their own 3D VRM avatar instance in browser or mobile. No shared state: your session, your agent.

Voice in, voice out

TTS and STT built in: talk to the agent, get spoken responses, set up trades in natural language.

Wallet-authenticated

Challenge signing binds the session to your Solana wallet: identity, balances and permissions without passwords.

Market surface

Price, chart, session high/low, market cap, volume and liquidity rendered next to the conversation.

Execution built-in

Pacifica execution flows directly inside the interface through the AIRewardrop builder code, with Jupiter and wider DeFi expansion on the path for $AIR3 holders.

Seeker-ready

Designed to land on Seeker smartphones: AIRi becomes the AIR3 mobile experience.

Session anatomy

What a session looks like.

Connect a wallet, sign the challenge, and the stage comes online: the avatar joins, market context loads, and the conversation drives everything else.

  1. Connect & signPhantom, Solflare, Ledger or Coinbase Wallet. The challenge signature opens your authenticated session.
  2. Ask for contextCharts, prices, fundamentals, sentiment or a full market read, spoken or typed.
  3. Shape the tradeThe agent renders the setup with entry, take profit and stop loss inside the session UX.
  4. Stay in the loopPortfolio views link back to AIRTrack; vault questions route to AIRTrading.
OriginPacifica Hackathon

AIRi was born as AIRifica, the AIRewardrop submission to the Pacifica Hackathon (March to April 2026): a per-user 3D VRM avatar with natural-language trade execution. The hackathon ended, the product stayed, and the name became simply AIRi.
