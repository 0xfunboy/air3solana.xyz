Every signal since the first tracked trade, timestamped with entry, exit and outcome. AIRTrack has no seasons and no deposits: it is the pure trading record.

Vault execution trades flow through the same lifecycle, and the weekly season reports on the AIRTrading side are built on this data.

Why proof matters
