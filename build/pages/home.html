How it works

One continuous trading workflow.

AIR3 collapses the fragmented crypto workflow into a single loop that runs every week.

SignalThe agent reads market data, social sentiment and onchain context.
AnalysisChart, setup, risk framework, trade logic and public commentary.
ExecutionAIRTrading routes approved vault execution through Pacifica.
TrackingAIRTrack records trade states, PnL, seasons and history.
SettlementPositive PnL routes into treasury, AIR3 buyback and pro-rata distribution.
