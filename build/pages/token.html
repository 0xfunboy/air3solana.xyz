Flywheel

Buyback

AIRTrading: profit → buyback

Positive weekly PnL routes to the AIR3 buyback at settlement, then splits 50% to users (pro rata to active capital) and 50% to treasury. Profitable seasons are structural buy pressure.

Burn-to-access

AIRTool: burn for deployments

Communities burn AIR3 (100K per agent instance) to deploy the agent in their Telegram or Discord. All burns are on-chain and public.

Sponsorship

AIRSponsor: segments paid in AIR3

Projects buy AIR3 to sponsor live analysis segments and branded drops inside the agent feed via buy-and-burn mechanics.

Earn

AIRSocial: engage to earn

Community missions and measurable engagement convert social activity into AIR3 rewards once AIRSocial launches.

Access

Holder thresholds

Holding AIR3 unlocks gated flows. For example, the AIRTrading whitelist qualifies wallets holding at least the AIR3 threshold (currently 1,000,000 AIR3; dApp values are authoritative).
